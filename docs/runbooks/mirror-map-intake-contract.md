# Mirror map — intake-contract rules in `docs/runbooks/fresh-isolated-pilot.md`

Maintained beside the runbook it indexes: the revision merged by PR #153 (`fe66a13`), which is frozen until
G1 closes. It does not amend the runbook; it is the checklist a runbook change must walk.

## How to use it

Before changing any rule below, open its owner and every mirror listed for it and change all of them in one
commit, or state in the PR why a mirror deliberately differs. The owner statement is the one other sections
must defer to; a mirror may restate for the operator's convenience but must not add or drop a condition.
Where a mirror already differs from its owner at this revision, the row's Notes say so for the differences
found in this review, and "Known drift at this revision" groups the principal ones. Neither is exhaustive: the
G1 closure PR re-checks each mirror against its owner rather than trusting this list.

Conventions:

- Section labels are the runbook's own: a § letter for a section, a step label for a checklist step (I13,
  I14, §G1d …), "§Q n" for numbered step n of §Q and "§Q STOP list" for the list that closes §Q. A step's
  STOP list belongs to its step. Line numbers are omitted on purpose; they drift.
- "§I definition block" = everything from the "Initial-intake contract — reusable definition" heading after
  I12 up to I13: the fenced definitions and the bold sub-blocks that follow them, named here after "›" (the
  "ONE rule" paragraph, "Per-leg result fields", "Checkable inequality", "The ONLY legitimate outcomes",
  "Stamp verification", "Leg-status truth", the PR #139 / #140 / #143 notes, "Mechanical side effects"). The
  block has no end marker; the runbook places the definition "before I13" (see the `INTAKE_CONTRACT_VERSION`
  row).
- "§I closure rule" = the runbook's cross-reference name for the text in item 2 ("Counted loud failure") of
  "The ONLY legitimate outcomes"; it has no heading of its own.
